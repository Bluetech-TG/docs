## About Us

HotelPilot Suite is a SaaS Property Management System (PMS) published by Bluetech TG, designed for hotels and hospitality groups to manage reservations, billing, and day-to-day operations.  
Contact: **bluetechtg@gmail.com**.

## Two Audiences Covered by this Policy

This policy applies to two distinct categories of data subjects:

- **Staff and Management of Subscribed Hotels** (administrators, front desk, housekeeping): they hold direct user accounts on HotelPilot Suite. For these records, **HotelPilot Suite acts as Data Controller**.
- **Hotel Guests and Travelers**: whose information (bookings, contact details, identity documents) is entered by hotel staff into the system. For this data, **the hotel remains Data Controller** and **HotelPilot Suite acts as Data Processor** under GDPR and applicable data protection frameworks.

## Data We Process

- **Staff User Accounts**: name, email, phone number, designated hotel property, role, hashed passwords, and two-factor authentication secrets (MFA/TOTP).
- **Hotel Subscription Billing Data**: tier, invoice records. Credit card numbers and Mobile Money credentials are **never stored on our servers**: transactions are handled directly by payment partners Stripe (card) and FedaPay (T-Money, Flooz, Orange Money).
- **Operational Data Entered by Hotel Staff**: reservations, guest folios, identity card photo captures taken during check-in, housekeeping statuses, internal notes, and staff audit logs.

## Hosting & Subprocessors

- **Application & Database Infrastructure**: Render (dedicated managed PostgreSQL instances).
- **Card Payments**: Stripe.
- **Mobile Money Payments**: FedaPay.
- **Mobile Companion Updates** (Expo).

These partners only process information strictly necessary for their services and are prohibited from secondary uses.

## Security

- End-to-end encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- Strict multi-tenant data isolation per property.
- Two-factor authentication available for staff accounts.
- Timestamped audit trails of administrative actions.

## Data Retention

Data is preserved for the duration of the hotel's active subscription. Upon termination, hotels may request full data export by writing to **bluetechtg@gmail.com**, subject to statutory accounting retention obligations (OHADA uniform act).

## Your Rights

To exercise rights of access, correction, or deletion regarding staff account data, contact **bluetechtg@gmail.com**.

Hotel guests wishing to exercise their privacy rights regarding personal data collected by a hotel should contact the hotel directly, as they are the primary Data Controller.

## Mobile Companion App

A mobile companion app for hotel staff (housekeeping updates, internal messaging, fast extra charging) is in development. This policy applies equally to the mobile companion.

## Children's Privacy

HotelPilot Suite is an enterprise B2B platform and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

## Contact

For privacy or data processing inquiries: **bluetechtg@gmail.com**.
