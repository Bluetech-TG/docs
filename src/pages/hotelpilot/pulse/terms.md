## Relationship to HotelPilot Suite

HotelPilot Pulse is a specialized field application designed for operational staff (housekeepers, valets, maintenance staff, receptionists, supervisors) of establishments holding an active HotelPilot Suite subscription. It operates in direct connection with your establishment's cloud PMS instance.

## Accounts and Authentication

- Access to HotelPilot Pulse requires valid user credentials issued by your hotel establishment administrator.
- Authentication tokens (JWT) are stored securely on the device using native encrypted storage (Keychain / EncryptedSharedPreferences).
- Users are responsible for preserving credential confidentiality. Sharing staff login credentials across shifts without authorization is prohibited.

## Usage and Operational Data

- HotelPilot Pulse processes operational data (room statuses, cleaning durations, maintenance incident photos, lost & found logs, guest check-in/out records).
- All photos taken through the application must strictly relate to hotel operations (damage reporting, lost items, room status). Capturing unauthorized personal photos of guests or private belongings is strictly forbidden.
- The subscribing hotel establishment remains the primary Data Controller for guest records.

## Offline Functionality and Local Queue

- HotelPilot Pulse includes local offline caching (SQLite) to allow seamless work in basement areas or dead zones.
- Pending modifications queue locally and replay automatically upon reconnecting.
- Users are advised to connect their device to a stable network at the end of their shift to ensure complete synchronization with the central PMS.
