## About Us

HotelPilot Pulse is a specialized mobile application published by Bluetech TG for hotel personnel operating with HotelPilot Suite.  
Contact: **bluetechtg@gmail.com**.

## Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how personal and operational data is handled within HotelPilot Pulse. The application connects directly to the backend servers of your hotel's HotelPilot Suite subscription.

## Data We Process

- **Staff Account Information**: username, email, assigned role, hotel property association, and encrypted authentication tokens (JWT).
- **Operational Data**: timestamps of cleaning sessions, room inspection approvals, room maintenance logs, lost & found entries, key tracking records.
- **Photos Taken by Staff**: photos attached to maintenance issues or lost items are uploaded directly and securely (HTTPS) to your hotel's dedicated cloud storage instance.
- **Device Logs & Push Notifications**: technical device tokens used exclusively to route operational push notifications (e.g. urgent room requests or internal messages).

## Permissions Requested on Your Device

- **Camera**: used strictly to take pictures of maintenance defects or lost & found items.
- **Photo Library**: optional, to attach a photo already taken to an issue.
- **Notifications**: used to receive urgent shift notifications and internal staff messages.

HotelPilot Pulse **does not** track continuous background GPS location and **never** sells or shares user data with third-party advertisers.

## Data Security & Offline Storage

- Communications between the app and the backend are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
- In offline mode, mutations are temporarily cached in an encrypted local database (SQLite) on the device until connectivity is restored.
- Authentication tokens are safeguarded in the hardware-backed secure store of the mobile device.

## Your Rights

Hotel employees may request access or correction of their personal profile data through their hotel administrator or by contacting **bluetechtg@gmail.com**.

## Contact

For privacy or data handling inquiries: **bluetechtg@gmail.com**.
